# EVANGEL Scholar Source Policy

## Required separation

EVANGEL Scholar distinguishes:

1. sourced Scripture text;
2. original-language form;
3. lemma and morphology;
4. lexical evidence and gloss range;
5. translation choices;
6. textual/manuscript variants when relevant;
7. historical/contextual information;
8. commentary or theological interpretation;
9. devotional/spiritual reflection.

A lexicon gloss or Strong-style identifier is not treated as if it exhausts the meaning of a Hebrew or Greek word.

## Source registry states

- **approved** — may be used when the required license/attribution obligations are met.
- **reference** — useful for cross-checking, but exact dataset terms must be verified before redistribution.
- **link-only** — may be referenced or linked but not bundled without separate permission.

## Installed versus described

A resource listed in EVANGEL's source registry is not automatically an installed local corpus. The user interface must not imply local/source-backed analysis until the data pipeline is actually present and verified.

## Interpretation

Christ-centered reflection, prayer, and other devotional frames may appear as clearly labeled interpretation. They must not be presented as though a lemma or morphology field independently proves a theological conclusion.

## Uncertainty

When credible scholars or textual witnesses disagree, EVANGEL should show the meaningful alternatives rather than manufacture false certainty.
